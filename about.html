About Bouldeer

Bouldeer is a free map of indoor climbing gyms for climbers who travel. Wherever you land, it shows the bouldering, top rope and lead walls nearby: more than 3,600 gyms in over 80 countries, each with its address, climbing types and directions, plus day-pass prices and opening hours where we have confirmed them.

Built for climbing on the road

  • Find a wall before you pack your shoes. Search by city, country or gym name, or open the map wherever you are, and filter by bouldering, top rope or lead.
  • Search in the local script. Type 成都, 東京 or 서울 as easily as Chengdu, Tokyo or Seoul. Where a gym has a name in its own language, Bouldeer shows it next to the English one.
  • Get there without a shared language. Many gyms show their address in the local script to hand to a taxi driver, with a copy button and directions in Google Maps, or Amap in mainland China.
  • Read it in your language. Bouldeer is available in English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.
  • Remember your trips. Sign in to save gyms, mark the ones you have climbed, check in to collect a passport stamp for each city and keep a logbook of your sessions.

Where the information comes from

Every gym is researched one by one from its own website and social accounts and from public map listings, and checked before it appears on the map. When we have not confirmed a detail, such as a price or opening hours, Bouldeer says so instead of guessing. If something is out of date, Suggest an edit or Report a problem on the gym’s page sends it to us, and every correction is checked before it goes live.

Who it’s for

Climbers who travel: on holiday, on a work trip, on a long trip through several countries, or new in town. You don’t need an account to use the map.

Questions

What is Bouldeer?
Bouldeer is a free website and web app that maps indoor climbing gyms worldwide, made for climbers who travel. It lists more than 3,600 bouldering, top rope and lead climbing gyms in over 80 countries.
How do I find a bouldering gym when I travel?
Open www.bouldeer.com, search for the city you are visiting (in English or the local script) or use your location, and filter by bouldering. Each gym page has the address, directions and, where confirmed, day-pass prices and opening hours.
Is Bouldeer free?
Yes. Bouldeer is free, shows no ads and needs no account to use the map. An account is only needed to save gyms, check in and keep a logbook.
Which countries does Bouldeer cover?
Over 80 countries on every continent, with the most gyms in the United States, China, Japan, Germany, France, the United Kingdom, South Korea, Canada, Spain and Australia.
Can I find climbing gyms in China?
Yes. Bouldeer lists nearly 500 climbing gyms in more than 40 Chinese cities, many with their Chinese name and address and a link to open the address in Amap.
Does Bouldeer sell day passes or take bookings?
No. Bouldeer is not connected to the gyms it lists and takes no bookings or payments. Buy passes and book directly with the gym.
Are prices and opening hours up to date?
Bouldeer shows only details it has confirmed and marks the rest as not confirmed yet. Gyms change their hours and prices, so check with the gym before you go.
Which languages is Bouldeer available in?
English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.
