About Bouldeer

A free, community-sourced map of indoor climbing gyms — bouldering, top rope, and lead — across 40+ countries, kept current by the people who climb at them.

How the map works

Each pin is colour-coded by what's on offer: indoor bouldering, top rope, or lead climbing. A pin split into wedges means a gym offers more than one. Use the region and type filters in the side panel to narrow the map, or search by gym name, suburb, or country. Sign in to filter to the gyms you've climbed or saved.

Where the data comes from

The starting dataset was researched and cross-checked gym by gym — names, suburbs, addresses, and what kind of climbing is actually on offer — rather than pulled from one source and trusted blindly. It's still not exhaustive, and gyms open, close, move, and rebrand. From here, the map grows and gets corrected by the community.

Adding and fixing gyms

  • Add a gym — drop a pin for any gym that's missing.
  • Edit this spot — on any pin, fix the name, location, climbing type, or add a photo link.
  • Report incorrect info — on any pin, flag something that looks wrong without editing it yourself.

New gyms and edits go into a review queue — a moderator approves or rejects them before they're visible to everyone else. An edited gym keeps showing its current details on the map until that edit is approved, so nothing unreviewed goes live in the meantime.

Tracking your own climbing

Sign in (Google or an email magic link) to mark gyms as climbed or saved, and keep a private logbook of sessions. These are private to your account — nobody else can see what you've climbed or saved.
