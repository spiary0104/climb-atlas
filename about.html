About Bouldeer

Bouldeer is a free map of indoor climbing gyms around the world (bouldering, top rope and lead), with thousands of gyms in 80+ countries, kept up to date by climbers.

What you can do

  • Find gyms on the map or by searching for a gym, city or country, and filter by climbing type.
  • Open a gym’s page for its address, climbing types and directions, or browse country by country in Regions.
  • Sign in to save gyms, mark the ones you’ve climbed, check in to collect passport stamps and keep a logbook of your sessions.

Kept up to date by climbers

The starting list was researched gym by gym, but gyms open, close and move. If you’re signed in you can:

  • Add a gym that’s missing.
  • Suggest an edit when a gym’s details are wrong.
  • Report a problem if something looks off and you’re not sure of the fix.

A moderator checks new gyms and edits before they appear on the map.

Who it’s for

Anyone who climbs indoors or wants to start: locals looking for a new wall and travellers looking for one on the road. You don’t need an account to use the map.
