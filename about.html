About Bouldeer

A free, community-sourced map of indoor climbing gyms — bouldering, top rope, and lead — with thousands of gyms in 80+ countries, kept current by the people who climb at them.

How the map works

Each pin is colour-coded by what’s on offer: indoor bouldering, top rope, or lead climbing. A pin split into wedges means a gym offers more than one. The list beside the map shows the gyms in view, and the type chips and the All filters sheet narrow it by climbing type and by the gyms you’ve saved or climbed. Search by gym name, suburb, city, or country to jump there.

Gym pages and Regions

Every gym has its own page with its details, a small map, directions, and a line showing who added it and when it was last edited. To browse by place, open Regions: pick a country, then a state or region, then a city.

Where the data comes from

The starting dataset was researched and cross-checked gym by gym — names, suburbs, addresses, and what kind of climbing is actually on offer — rather than pulled from one source and trusted blindly. It’s still not exhaustive, and gyms open, close, move, and rebrand. From here, the map grows and gets corrected by the community.

Adding and fixing gyms

  • Add a gym — place a pin for any gym that’s missing and fill in the details.
  • Suggest an edit — on any gym, fix the name, location, climbing type, or add a photo link.
  • Report a problem — flag something that looks wrong without editing it yourself.

You need to be signed in to add, suggest, or report. New gyms and edits go into a review queue — a moderator approves or rejects them before they’re visible to everyone else. An edited gym keeps showing its current details on the map until that edit is approved, so nothing unreviewed goes live in the meantime.

Tracking your own climbing

Sign in (Google or an email magic link) to mark gyms as climbed or saved, check in at a gym to collect a stamp in your passport, and keep a logbook of your sessions. These are private to your account — nobody else can see what you’ve climbed, saved, or logged. Read more in the Privacy Policy.
