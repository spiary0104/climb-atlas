Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Bouldeer is a free, community-sourced map of climbing gyms. This page says what the site handles, who can see it, which other services receive requests, and how to have your account removed.

Using the map without an account

You can browse the map, search, open gym pages and explore the Regions pages without signing in. Bouldeer does not set advertising or analytics cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. The hosting and map services listed below still see the ordinary technical details of a request, such as your IP address and browser type.

Your account

To save gyms, keep a logbook, check in, or submit anything, you sign in with an email magic link or with Google. Sign-in is handled by Supabase, our authentication and database provider. It holds your email address and, if you use Google, the basic profile details Google shares with it, along with sign-in records. Your email address is never displayed on Bouldeer.

You can also set a display name on your Me page. It is optional, 2 to 40 characters, and cannot contain an @ sign or angle brackets.

What is public

  • Approved gym information: name, location, address, climbing types, notes and photo link. Once a moderator approves it, it is part of the shared map. Do not put personal information in these fields.
  • Attribution: on a gym you added, the gym page says “added by” followed by your display name, or “a climber” if you have not set one. Gym pages also show how many people have contributed and when the gym was last edited. Display names are public.
  • Not public: your email address, your marks, check-ins, logbook, problem reports, and your contribution points and level (visible to you and to moderators).

Submissions

Adding a gym, suggesting an edit and reporting a problem all require you to be signed in, and each is limited per day to keep spam down. New gyms and edits are reviewed by moderators before they go live. Moderators can see the submission and which account sent it. If a submission is rejected, it is kept with the reason so you can see it on your Me page.

Problem reports are visible only to moderators and are never shown on the map. Photos are added as a link, not uploaded to Bouldeer. When a gym with a photo link is shown, your browser loads that photo directly from the site it is hosted on, and Bouldeer asks the browser not to send a referrer.

Private to your account

  • Marks: which gyms you saved or climbed.
  • Check-ins and your passport: the gym, the time and an optional note of up to 140 characters. Passport stamps and milestones are worked out from these.
  • Logbook: session date, gym, mood, notes, and the climbs you log (type, grade, attempts, whether you sent it).

These are stored against your account and protected by database access rules, so only you can read them through Bouldeer. Other climbers and moderators cannot. The person who runs the site has administrator access to the database and uses it to run and secure the service. The share card you can make from a check-in is drawn on your own device and goes nowhere unless you choose to share it.

Location

Bouldeer asks your browser for your location only when you use something that needs it, and your browser asks you first. You can say no and everything else still works.

  • Locate button on the map: centres the map on you and sorts the list by distance.
  • Use my location on the Add a gym page: moves the map pin to where you are.
  • Check in: on phones and other touch devices, your position is compared with the gym’s, and you must be within about 500 metres. “Check in nearby” finds the closest gym the same way.

Your position is used in your browser and is not sent to Bouldeer or saved. A check-in stores the gym, the time and your note, not where you were. If you submit a new gym, the pin position you chose for that gym is saved, because that is the gym’s location.

Stored in your browser

Bouldeer does not set cookies. It stores the following on your device, and clearing the site’s data in your browser removes all of it.

  • Your sign-in session, kept by Supabase in local storage, so you stay signed in.
  • A draft of the Add a gym form, until you submit or discard it.
  • Your list layout choice, the last map position you viewed and your recent searches.
  • A flag in session storage so a milestone message is shown at most once per browser session.
  • Service worker caches for offline use: the app’s own files, the code libraries and fonts below, map tiles you have viewed, and the public list of approved gyms. Private data such as your marks, check-ins and logbook is not cached this way.

Services that receive requests

Loading Bouldeer means your browser contacts the services below. Each one sees your IP address and browser details, and they have their own privacy policies. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

  • Supabase: the database and sign-in. It receives your sign-in details, your submissions, marks, check-ins and logbook, and sends the sign-in email.
  • Vercel: hosts the site. Its server logs record requests, including your IP address, browser type and the pages requested.
  • Google: receives a sign-in request only if you choose Google sign-in. Google Fonts also serves the site’s fonts to every visitor. If you tap Directions on a gym, Google Maps opens with the gym’s coordinates.
  • unpkg.com and cdn.jsdelivr.net: deliver the code libraries Bouldeer uses for the map and for talking to Supabase.
  • CARTO: provides the map style, tiles and labels, built on OpenStreetMap data. It sees which part of the map you are viewing.
  • Sites hosting contributed photos: as described under Submissions.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. There is no delete button in the app yet. To delete your account, email hello@bouldeer.com from the address you signed in with, and we will handle it within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes your profile and display name, your marks, your check-ins and your logbook. Gym information you contributed that was already approved stays on the map, without any link back to you. Copies held in our providers’ logs and backups expire on their own schedules.

To correct or remove something on a gym page, use Suggest an edit or Report a problem on that page, or email us.

Age

You must be at least 13 to create an account. Bouldeer is not aimed at children, and if we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13 we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, by emailing us. You can also decline location, sign out, or clear your browser’s site data at any time.

Changes

If Bouldeer starts collecting something new, such as analytics or advertising, this page will be updated first and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions or requests: hello@bouldeer.com. See also the Terms of Service.
