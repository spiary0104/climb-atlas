Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Bouldeer is a small, free community map of climbing gyms. We collect as little as we can, we don’t use ads or analytics, and we don’t sell your data.

Without an account

You can use the map, search and gym pages without signing in. Bouldeer sets no cookies and uses no tracking. The services that deliver the site (listed below) see ordinary request details such as your IP address and browser.

With an account

You sign in with an email link or with Google, through Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. We store:

  • Your email address (and, if you use Google, the basic profile details Google shares) so you can sign in. It is never shown on Bouldeer.
  • An optional display name, shown publicly as “added by …” on gyms you add.
  • Your saved and climbed gyms, check-ins and logbook: the gyms, dates, notes, moods and grades you enter.
  • What you submit: new gyms, suggested edits and problem reports, linked to your account.

Who can see what

  • Everyone: approved gym details, and your display name on gyms you added. Don’t put personal information in gym details.
  • Moderators: submissions and reports, and which account sent them, so they can review them.
  • Only you: your email address, saved and climbed gyms, check-ins, logbook and contribution points.

Private data is protected by database access rules. The person who runs Bouldeer has administrator access to the database and uses it only to run and secure the site.

Location

Bouldeer asks for your location only when you use the locate button, “Use my location” on Add a gym, or check in on a phone (you need to be within about 500 metres of the gym). Your browser asks first, and you can say no. Your position is used on your device and is never sent to us or saved: a check-in records the gym and the time, not where you were.

Stored on your device

No cookies. Your browser’s storage keeps your sign-in session, an unsent Add a gym draft, your list layout, last map position and recent searches, a note so a milestone message shows once per visit, and offline copies of the site, viewed map tiles and the public gym list. Clearing the site’s data in your browser removes all of it.

Services we use

Each of these sees your IP address and browser details and has its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase: database and sign-in. It stores your account data and sends sign-in emails.
  • Vercel: hosts the site and keeps request logs.
  • Google: Google sign-in (only if you choose it), Google Fonts, and Google Maps when you tap Directions.
  • CARTO: map tiles, built on OpenStreetMap data.
  • unpkg.com and cdn.jsdelivr.net: the code libraries for the map and the database connection.
  • Photo hosts: gym photos are links, so your browser loads them from the site that hosts them.

Deleting your account

Email hello@bouldeer.com from the address you sign in with and we’ll delete your account within 30 days. That removes your email address, display name, saved and climbed gyms, check-ins, logbook and points. Gyms, edits and reports you submitted stay, with the link to you removed. Copies in our providers’ logs and backups expire on their own schedules.

You can also email us to ask what we hold about you or to have it corrected. To fix a gym’s details, use Suggest an edit or Report a problem on its page.

Changes

If Bouldeer starts collecting anything new, we’ll update this page first and change the date above.
