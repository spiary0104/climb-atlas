Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Bouldeer is a small, free map of climbing gyms for climbers who travel, run from New South Wales, Australia. We collect as little as we can. We don’t show ads, we don’t use analytics, and we don’t sell or share your data for advertising.

Without an account

You can use the map, search and gym pages without signing in. Bouldeer sets no cookies and doesn’t track you. The services that deliver the site (listed below) see ordinary request details such as your IP address and browser.

With an account

You need to be at least 16 to create an account. You sign in with an email link or with Google, through our sign-in provider, Supabase. We store:

  • Your email address, so you can sign in. If you use Google, Google also shares your basic profile (name and profile picture) with our sign-in provider. None of this is shown on Bouldeer.
  • An optional display name.
  • Your saved and climbed gyms, check-ins (the gym, the time, and an optional short note or photo link) and your logbook (dates, climbs, grades, moods and notes).
  • What you send us: new gyms, suggested edits and problem reports, linked to your account.

We use this to run your account and the features you choose. We also use it to check and publish gym information and to keep the site free of abuse.

Who can see what

  • Everyone: approved gym details, and your display name on gyms you added (shown as “added by …”). Display names aren’t listed anywhere else: only you and moderators can read your profile. Don’t put personal information in gym details.
  • Moderators: what you send us, the display name of the account that sent it, and contribution points, so they can check submissions. They never see your email.
  • Only you: your email address, saved and climbed gyms, check-ins and logbook.

Private data is protected by database access rules and travels encrypted (HTTPS). The person who runs Bouldeer has administrator access to the database and uses it only to run and secure the site.

Location

Bouldeer asks for your location only when you use the locate button, “Use my location” on Add a gym, or check in on a phone (you need to be within about 500 metres of the gym). Your browser asks first, and you can say no.

  • Locate button and check-in: your position is used on your device only. It’s never sent to us or saved. A check-in records the gym and the time, not where you were.
  • Add a gym: “Use my location” moves the pin to where you are. The pin you submit is sent to us as the gym’s location and is published once approved, so move it onto the gym first.

Stored on your device

No cookies. Your browser’s storage keeps:

  • your sign-in session;
  • an unsent Add a gym draft, including the pin position;
  • your list layout, map style, last map view, recent searches and chosen language;
  • notes so the intro, the welcome and milestone messages show only once;
  • offline copies of the site, map images you’ve viewed and the public gym list.

Signing out or deleting your account ends your session. Clearing the site’s data in your browser removes everything else.

Where your data is processed

Your account data is stored in Singapore. Our providers also process data in the United States and other countries. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase: database and sign-in; your account data is stored in Singapore.
  • Resend: delivers sign-in emails, so it sees your email address. It stores data in the United States and keeps email logs for 30 days.
  • Vercel: hosts the site worldwide and keeps request logs.
  • CARTO: map images, built on OpenStreetMap data. It shortens IP addresses and keeps its logs for 30 days in the United States.
  • Google: Google sign-in (only if you choose it), Google Fonts, and Google Maps when you tap Directions.
  • Amap: only when you choose to open an address in Amap.
  • unpkg.com and cdn.jsdelivr.net: the code libraries for the map and the database connection.
  • Photo hosts: gym photos are links, so your browser loads them from the site that hosts them.

How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Gyms, edits and reports you sent: kept as part of the map. After you delete your account they’re no longer linked to you.
  • Providers’ logs: Resend and CARTO keep logs for 30 days. Vercel and Supabase keep request logs for periods they set.

Deleting your account

Use Delete account at the bottom of your Me page to delete your account straight away. You can do this in any browser at bouldeer.com: sign in, open Me, and choose Delete account. The steps are also on Delete your account.

Deleting removes your email address, display name, saved and climbed gyms, check-ins, logbook and points. Gyms, edits and reports you sent stay on the map, including any text you wrote, but they’re no longer linked to you.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or export your data, or object to how we use it: email hello@bouldeer.com. To fix a gym’s details, use Suggest an edit or Report a problem on its page. If you’re unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au), your local data protection authority in the EU, or the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Bouldeer isn’t designed for children. You need to be at least 16 to create an account.

Changes

If Bouldeer starts collecting anything new, we’ll update this page first and change the date above. For significant changes we’ll also post a notice on the site.

Contact

hello@bouldeer.com. See also the Terms of Service.
