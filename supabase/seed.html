Boulder Atlas — one-time seed

Loads every spot from js/data.js and upserts them into your Supabase spots table. Safe to run more than once — it matches on id, so re-running just re-syncs the seed rows rather than duplicating them.

Before clicking the button: run supabase/schema.sql in your Supabase SQL Editor, and make sure js/supabase-init.js has your real project URL and anon key filled in (this page loads that same file).
