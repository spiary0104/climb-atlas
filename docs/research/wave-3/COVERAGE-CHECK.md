# Wave 3C: coverage-gap check of established markets (2026-10-02)

Quick, country-level check of countries left out of the global gap analysis because Bouldeer already has more than 10 gyms. **No gyms were researched for import; no sections, batches or production changes.** Bouldeer counts come from the committed match index (2,127 gyms, equal to production at the gap analysis). Method per country: best aggregate sources for an independent total, a sample of 15–28 independently listed gyms checked against Bouldeer (sample ratio), an indicative gap range, evidence confidence and research difficulty. Raw records with every source and sampled gym: `coverage/*.json`.

**Read before acting on a number:** totals that include rope-only walls are upper bounds; sample ratios are flattered by gyms in cities Bouldeer already covers (the gap range is the better figure); some sampled gyms were confirmed via directories or chain lists rather than their own sites; operating status was not checked per gym; counts are lower-effort estimates (search quotas ran out in places). Korea has no national count.

Shortlist rule: 1 = recommended now, confidence ≥ medium, difficulty ≤ medium, Bouldeer ≤ 50; 2 = recommended now with Bouldeer > 50 (needs region sections); 3 = recommended now but low confidence or high difficulty; 4 = later or not needed. Within a group: by gap midpoint.
