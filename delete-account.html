Delete your account

Last updated: 26 November 2026

You can delete your Bouldeer account yourself at any time, from the website or the app. It takes effect straight away.

How to delete your account

  1. Go to bouldeer.com in any browser (or open the Bouldeer app) and sign in with the email address or Google account you use for Bouldeer.
  2. Open Me.
  3. At the bottom of the page, choose Delete account, tick the box to confirm, and press the button.

You don’t need to install anything: signing in on the website works the same as in the app.

What’s deleted

Your email address and sign-in details, display name, saved and climbed gyms, check-ins, logbook and points.

What stays, and why

Gyms, edits and problem reports you sent stay on the map, including any text you wrote, because other climbers rely on them. They’re no longer linked to you. Our providers’ short-lived logs expire on their own schedules (Resend and CARTO keep logs for 30 days).

Questions

Email hello@bouldeer.com. See also the Privacy Policy.
