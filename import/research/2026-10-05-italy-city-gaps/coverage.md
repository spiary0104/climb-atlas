# Coverage: Italy, Torino / Bologna / Palermo / Napoli gaps (2026-10-05)

## Result
- 15 candidates (it-001 to it-015), 16 sources registered. Importer check: ready 5 | review 6 | blocked 4 | existing 0 (one review candidate, Presa B+, is the same gym as an existing record).
- The earlier timeouts did not recur: every official site below loaded on 2026-10-05, except guessed hostnames (palaroccia.it, demonrockwall.it, bluerockclimbing.it, freeclimbingpalermo.it) that did not respond; the real sites are freeclimbingnapoli.it and freeclimbingpalermo.wordpress.com.

## What was checked
- Torino: SASP, BoulderBar, Bside, Escape Climbing Garden (Collegno), La Mole Sports Academy (Rivoli), Centro Arrampicata Torino. Own sites fetched; OSM (Overpass sport=climbing, bbox 44.9-45.25 N / 7.45-7.85 E) for pins.
- Bologna: Presa B+, UP Urban Climbing, Level24 (Casalecchio di Reno), Monkeys' Planet and Eden Park (San Lazzaro di Savena). Own sites fetched; pins from the gyms' own map links or Nominatim/OSM.
- Palermo: SCALART, Free Climbing Palermo.
- Napoli: Palaroccia Napoli (Quarto, also known as DEMON Rock Wall / FreeClimbing Napoli), BlueRock (Torre Annunziata, official Instagram only).
- Directories used only to enumerate: infoboulder.com, oliunid, rutavertical-style lists, falesia.it, Google/Bing-style searches.

## Not candidates / left out
- Out of the four metro areas: Boulder Garage (Cumiana), Blockbusters (Mondovi), Vertigo (Cuneo), Inout Le Vele (Chiusa di Pesio), Infinity Boulder (Trento), Quincinetto and Priero boulder halls (Piemonte, far from Torino).
- Pozzuoli Boulder (Via Napoli 19, Pozzuoli): only directory pages (palestralecolonne.it, falesia.it), no official site or social found, and the address has two streets of that name; see unlocated.md.
- DEMON Rock Wall at Palasport Trincone, Monterusciello (Via Cosimo Luigi Miccoli 6): older small wall (about 80 m2) of the same operator as Palaroccia; its Facebook page was not re-read, and it may have moved to Quarto, so no separate candidate.
- Napoli city itself (Vomero, Fuorigrotta, Scampia etc.): searches found no further bouldering gym with a primary source. Project Y (ex OPG) was seen only as a directory listing.
- Palermo metro (Bagheria, Mondello): nothing found.
- Torino: Vertical/CUS Torino and other university or municipal walls were not researched.
