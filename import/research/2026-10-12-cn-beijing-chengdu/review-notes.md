# Review notes: China Beijing and Chengdu coverage gap (2026-10-12-cn-beijing-chengdu)

Reviewer: Claude (AI) on the owner's instruction; owner sign-off pending. Tally: accept 13 | same-as 0 | defer 3 | reject 0 (16 candidates).

## Method
Same as the Kunming and Changsha section (`2026-10-12-cn-kunming-changsha/review-notes.md`): Amap listing lists for Beijing (about 90 climbing-named listings) and Chengdu (about 47) were compared with the 55 Beijing and 30 Chengdu gyms already in Bouldeer by name, address and approximate position; every listing that is not in Bouldeer and is not a children's school, public or school wall, outdoor crag or fitness club was examined through its RedNote account (or visitor notes). Accept rule: the gym's own RedNote account has a 2026 post about this gym, bouldering is shown in the gym's own text (bio, V-graded route posts, bouldering area resets or contests) and the Amap listing is live. A RedNote map of Chengdu's gyms (2026-09 edition, 30 gyms) was used to cross-check openings and closures. Names: `English (Branch) [中文]`. Pins: entrance coordinate where the listing has one (`building`), otherwise the single Amap pin reading (`street`, flagged; about 100 m); see the Kunming notes for why.
