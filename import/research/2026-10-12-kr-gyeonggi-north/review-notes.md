## Method
Candidates were found with Naver Place list search and Kakao Map search (about 120 city, district, neighbourhood and station names, each with 클라이밍, 볼더링, 클라이밍짐, 클라이밍센터, 암장 and similar keywords, plus about 50 chain and brand names), then each gym's own Naver Place listing was read (price list, booking, gym-authored feed posts) and its owner-uploaded photos and post images were inspected as montages. Bouldering is accepted only where the gym's own photos or posts show mat-floored bouldering walls. Open status rests on gym-authored 2026 posts, a live booking item and a price list; visitor reviews and Place clips dated 2026-09/10 are only corroboration. Pins are the gym's own Naver pin (`map-service-pin`, building), cross-checked with Kakao (all accepted gyms within 20 m of the Kakao pin).
