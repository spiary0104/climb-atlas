- Open: https://boulderspot.sk/kontakt/ and https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1011420885 (the Squarebizz Bory estate); optionally Google Maps, search "Spot Climbing gym Bratislava".
- Question: where exactly is the Spot building ("objekt C", Squarebizz Bory), as a coordinate (the gym's pin on Google Maps or the building in OSM)?
- Found: the gym looks open with bouldering (opened Nov 2024, about 1000 m2, Kilter and campus board; events and courses listed for autumn 2026). But the pin taken from the gym's embedded map (48.2113 N, 16.9967 E) is about 1.2 km west of Squarebizz Bory, beside a police school, so it is wrong. OSM has no element for the gym; the estate polygon centre (48.2146 N, 17.0124 E) is only area-level. The candidate was changed to area precision (blocked).
