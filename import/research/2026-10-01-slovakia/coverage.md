# Slovakia coverage (wave 3A, 2026-10-01)

Candidates: 22 (sk-001 to sk-022; 20 with bouldering "yes", 2 "unknown"). Cities covered with a primary page: Bratislava (Block Dock Raca and Petrzalka, Spot, Fanatix, Vertigo, K2), Zilina (K2, La Skala), Kosice (Rozlomity, T2, Steam Factory), Liptovsky Mikulas, Zvolen, Poprad, Tatranska Lomnica, Turcianske Jaseno, Stara Lubovna, Trnava, Nitra, Trencin, Presov, Malacky.

Main sources: gym websites (fetched directly), the OSM Overpass extract (coordinates for most), the gym-published site map data for Spot (embed centre), Fanatix and Steam Factory (site location values), and two directories used only as leads: zagurami.eu wall list and the SHS James federation list.

Checks: check.js reports ready 8, review 9, blocked 4, existing 1, invalid 0. Not changed to remove flags. Blocked ones: WoodRock (bouldering unknown), Stienka and Trencin (status not confirmed), Malina Malacky (municipal hall, bouldering unknown).

Probably missing: Banska Bystrica Brutal Povala (no coordinates, see unlocated.md); Pavucik; small school, club and municipal walls (Zlate Moravce, Smizany, Michalovce, Prievidza, Rozsnava, Svidnik, Cadca, Povazska Bystrica HK Manin, Ziar nad Hronom, Banska Stiavnica). These were seen only in directories or were not verified for public access and bouldering. Rope-only or outdoor walls (MY WALL Presov, Rokodromo Kosice, outdoor profiles, rope parks) were left out. Gymstar Kosice is mainly a fitness centre; its page did not show a bouldering offer, so it was excluded.

Weak spots to review: Spot coordinates come from an embedded map centre (precision set to street, flagged weak-coordinates); several gyms show live hours but no dated 2025-2026 post (Block Dock, Fanatix, Brutalka, Rozlomity); Pralez and Presov addresses come from directory snippets; Block Dock Raca matches an existing Bouldeer seed, as do Vertigo and K2 Zilina; Ovcin matched an existing seed 479 m away. Possible duplicate flags between Block Dock sites, and between Spot, Fanatix and K2 Bratislava, are just proximity in Bratislava.

Access problems: the shared web-search budget ran out part-way (some follow-up searches not done); Facebook pages (BouldroFka, Rozsnava) unreadable; T2 and mywall.sk sites are script-rendered or unreachable; t2boulder content read from the page bundle. No geocoder or Overpass call was made by me.
