# Israel coverage (wave 1, 2026-10-01)

**Cities with candidates:** Tel Aviv, Jerusalem, Haifa, Beer Sheva, Petah Tikva, Rehovot, Netanya, Rishon LeZion, Kibbutz Afikim (Jordan Valley). 12 candidates, all with a bouldering offer shown on a primary source and a coordinate source (3 from OSM nodes, 9 from official pins on the gyms' own sites).

**Main sources:** the Israel Climbing Association wall list (the most complete single list, used for discovery and addresses), each gym's own site, the Performance Rock, The Bloc and Urban Climbing chain pages, official Waze/Google pins on gym sites (one Waze short link resolved to coordinates), and the OSM extract.

**Unlocated (17):** see unlocated.md. Most Israeli gym sites publish only Waze place ids or Google search links, and OSM has no element for them. These are real, mostly open gyms (Isaac, Desert Climbing, The Wall Modiin, Boulder Haifa, Totem, Rujum, Boulderland Kfar Saba, iClimb branches and others) that need a coordinate pass.

**Checked and left out:**
- VKING Raanana: closed (Tel Aviv branch is the only one on the site).
- Performance Rock Modiin: on the federation list but the chain site no longer shows it (page not found).
- Rockiz Holon: open-air rope park; Sky Town Tel Aviv: rope/extreme park; Gecko (Ramat Yishai): site text only mentions boulder as search keywords, federation says lead; iClimb Tel Aviv and Ashkelon: kids/rope park, no bouldering shown; Cliff (OSM node 6745095897): its domain no longer resolves; Ninja Park Rehovot, municipal hall walls (Tel Mond, HaOgen, Mateh Yehuda), Top 94 Eilat: rope or non-bouldering.
- OSM climbing elements for Shilat, Beit Oren, Alonim, Nahal Boker and similar are outdoor crags, ignored. Several unnamed OSM climbing sports_centre buildings (e.g. ways 1184539796, 1355474438, 1469148473/4) could not be tied to a named gym, so they were not used.

**Probably missing:** small kibbutz and school walls, and any gym opened in the last year. Hebrew Facebook/Instagram-only gyms are the likeliest gaps. The Bloc says it runs three gyms in Jerusalem and Tel Aviv but only two addresses were found.

**Access problems:** vengavenga.co.il blocks plain scripted requests (read with a browser user agent); cliffclimbing.co.il and emunimgym.com did not resolve/load; tzomet-ran.co.il was behind a bot check. Vking's two published pins differ by about 500 m (Waze pin used). The Bloc Jerusalem and Tel Aviv coordinates rest on OSM nodes whose names match, not on an official pin.
