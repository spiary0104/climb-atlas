- Same-website flags (Planet Rock x3, Adventure Rock x3, Odyssey x2, MW Climbing x2, Boulders vs its closed East gym): branches of one operator, each with its own address; reviewed_against lists the siblings.

- us-wi-005 Milwaukee Turners: public climbing gym in Turner Hall (day pass, two bouldering areas). Pin is street-level (the geocoder has no building for the address); check the pin if it matters.
- us-mi-003 Planet Rock Grand Rapids: pin is street-level (Union Ave SE), single chain page as source (Planet Rock's locations page lists hours and a dedicated bouldering room).
- us-in-006 Climb Lafayette: no OSM element or Nominatim match for 4650 Dale Dr (Nominatim also rate-limited the research machine for the later passes), so the pin is the US Census geocoder's address match, coord_precision street; the gym's own site gives address, hours, day pass for the bouldering and top-rope wall.
- us-oh-007 RockQuest: single source flag; the gym's own site states hours, address and a bouldering orientation.
- us-wi-003 / us-wi-004 Adventure Rock Milwaukee and Brookfield: bouldering rests on the chain-wide first-time sentence ("ropeless bouldering area" at any location); only Walker's Point is labelled "Bouldering" on its own.
- us-ks-001 RoKC Olathe and us-mo-004 RoKC North KC: bouldering rests on the chain About page (bouldering area, no weight limit); RoKC Underground is deferred because even that is not stated for it.
