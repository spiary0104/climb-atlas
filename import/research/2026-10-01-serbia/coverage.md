# Coverage: Serbia (RS), wave 3A, 2026-10-01

## Candidates (5)
Belgrade: Sektor44 (Novi Beograd), SKC Baza Boulder Gym (Stari Grad), Pulse Climbing Hub (Senjak, bouldering unknown, top-rope only confirmed). Novi Sad: Adrenalin. Nis: PAEK bouldering hall (club).

## Unlocated (5, see unlocated.md)
Uzice (SPK Stari Grad), Belgrade (Gekon, Granit, PK Beograd in Zemun, Black Rock school wall). These are club or school halls with no OSM element and no readable official map.

## Main sources
Official Instagram profiles (readable without login, with bio hours and dated posts), adrenalin.org.rs, paek-nis.org.rs, OSM extract (RS.json), SerbianClimbing.com directory (used as secondary). Third-party pages (Chalk Rebels, Yandex Maps, localgymsandfitness, company register) only used to cross-check.

## Checked and excluded
- Silosi Utopia, Dunavski kej, Belgrade: outdoor 28 m rope wall, no bouldering.
- SPK Vertikal / Ada Ciganlija wall and OSM "Avantura park": outdoor towers, rope.
- Nacionalni penjacki centar, Kosutnjak (Kneza Viseslava 72): state training centre with walls that are partly outdoor (closed in bad weather, OSM tags outdoor=yes); a bouldering wall exists per the institute, but access is by pre-arranged schedule with an instructor and no indoor/public status was confirmed. Worth a human look.
- V1 (Belgrade), Grdoba (Valjevo), Uzice crag sectors, Lipovac via ferrata and other OSM climbing nodes: outdoor crags.
- Arena Fun Factory (Belgrade): rope wall in an entertainment park, no bouldering.
- School and club rope walls without a bouldering offer found: PSK Spartak Subotica (10 m school wall), Kragujevac (Jovan Popovic school wall, club Gora), Kikinda (Pentraks, Save Tekelije 9), Kruševac (VMN Krstic hall), Pancevo (Soko), Cacak (temporary wall only), Kladovo (Karatas camp wall), Vrnjacka Banja (park wall, outdoor). No bouldering evidence, so not candidates; the clubs may be worth a later pass.
- Kraljevo: nothing found.
- Kosovo: out of scope for RS. No Kosovo gym recorded; none was researched.

## Probably missing
Small club and school halls in Vojvodina and central Serbia that only post on Facebook or Instagram; possible boulder walls in Kragujevac, Subotica, Kikinda and Pancevo clubs; Pulse's possible second location in Zemun; whether the Kosutnjak multifunctional hall (OSM way/208491262) has an indoor climbing wall.
