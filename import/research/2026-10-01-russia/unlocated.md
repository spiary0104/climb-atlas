# Russia: gyms without a candidate (no coordinate source, unverifiable, or no region code)

Evidence URL is the page that shows the gym exists. Directory pages (climbcomps.ru, maps.climbingpro.ru, mosclimbing.ru) are leads only.

## Coded cities: real or likely gyms, no usable coordinate source or no reachable primary source

### Moscow (MOSKVA)
- Red Point (RedPoint), ul. Vyatskaya 27 str. 12, Moscow, http://redpoint.msk.ru/ . OSM has the gym (node/2828435634, 55.79708, 37.58103, hours Mo-Fr 09-23). The official site refused connections from the research environment, so no primary evidence of bouldering or opening. Federation list calls it bouldering+difficulty: https://mosclimbing.ru/map
- Gravitatsiya, ul. Novoostapovskaya 5 str. 2, Moscow, https://gravitacia-club.ru/ . Site is behind a bot challenge (403), not bypassed. Same address as Sportstation (ru-015). Directory: https://maps.climbingpro.ru/city/moskva/
- HighWall, ul. Vorotynskaya 18, Moscow, http://highwall.ru/ (domain does not resolve) and https://highwall.wixsite.com/mysite (404). Listed as rope + bouldering by https://mosclimbing.ru/map . Probably closed or moved.
- BMSTU "GoClimb" (Baumanka), Gospitalnaya nab. 4/2, Moscow, http://goclimb.ru/ru/ . University hall with a bouldering room (site lists it); no coordinates on site, no OSM node found. Evidence: http://goclimb.ru/ru/
- Boulder Park Orekhovo, Moscow south-east (OSM node/5006420567, 55.60039, 37.70573, weekends 11-19), http://boulderpark.ru/ (does not resolve). Possibly seasonal or outdoor.
- Skala City, Kutuzovsky pr. 36 str. 14, https://skala-city.ru/ (OSM node/2828503040). Site shows no bouldering mention (appears rope-only); not checked as confirmed rope-only.
- Marina Club climbing wall, Leningradskoye sh. 25a, OSM node/2903803382; its page returns 410 Gone, probably closed.
- Edelweiss club, 2-ya Sinichkina 19, http://www.edelweyss.ru/ ; club with limited hours (17:00-21:00), no bouldering mention found.
- ProClimb, Vyatskaya 27 str. 12, https://proclimb.ru/ ; training club in the same building as Red Point, no bouldering mention found.
