# Peru: real gyms without a usable coordinate source (not in candidates.ndjson)

No OSM element exists in the extract for these, and the gym's own site/social shows no coordinates. A later pass can find a pin on the gym's own map link or a reliable source.

## Strong (primary source shows bouldering)

1. **Base Camp Peru**, Surquillo, Lima. Av. Tomas Marsano 630, Surquillo. Website https://basecampperu.com/ (pages /nosotros/, /contacto/). Own site (copyright 2026) describes multiple boulders of varied angles, publishes winter/summer hours and phone +51 981 265 252; no map embed or coordinates on the pages checked. Instagram instagram.com/basecampperu. Likely already on Bouldeer (reconcile will tell once located). Listed in the FEDPE rocodromo sheet.
2. **Bloque**, San Isidro, Lima. Instagram https://www.instagram.com/bloque.pe/ bio says private boulder wall plus Kilterboard, posts through Sept 2026; link-in-bio is a Linktree with WhatsApp +51 981 054 760 and a registration form, no address. Directory (climbing-gyms.com, not primary) gives Av. Dos de Mayo 127, San Isidro. bloque.pe site is a 2024 placeholder. Private/reservation access (check whether public).
3. **Ascenso Gimnasio de Escalada**, Tarapoto (San Martin). Instagram https://www.instagram.com/ascenso.pe/ bio: boulder and sport wall, Jr. Bugambilias 210, Mon-Sat 4-10 pm; latest post Sept 2026. No map link seen. Listed in FEDPE sheet.
4. **Rocodromo Boulder Calca**, Calca (Cusco). Instagram https://www.instagram.com/rocodromo.calca/ bio: boulder, training, yoga, classes; last visible post Oct 2023, so operating status unconfirmed. Facebook page linked in bio. Listed in FEDPE sheet.
5. **Banana Muro / Banana HUB**, La Molina, Lima. Instagram https://www.instagram.com/bananamuro/ (shown as Banana HUB) bio: private boulder wall, private classes by reservation, Rinconada del Lago area; last visible post Dec 2024, status unconfirmed. Older listings give Jr. Michigan 740, La Molina (phone +51 959 615 539); bananamuro.com no longer resolves in DNS. Possible relocation/rename. Listed in FEDPE sheet. Private access.

12. **La Escalada Escuela y Entrenamiento**, Cusco. Facebook only (unreadable); snippet says boulder area plus exercise area.
13. **El Boulder**, Barranco, Lima (Pedro de Osma 135, courtyard of Casa Drama). Old Topos Peru page; elboulder.com does not resolve. Likely closed or relocated; status unknown. Possibly the same team as another Lima gym.
14. **Muro Pitumarca / 7a Escalada**, Cusco region. Former 7a Escuela de Escalada basement boulder gym (Coliseo Cerrado, Wanchaq, Cusco) is described as moving to Pitumarca; 7aescalada.com lists Sector Yanama, Pitumarca (appears to be outdoor climbing parks). Not confirmed as an indoor gym.
15. **Polideportivo Villa El Salvador (Legado)**, Lima. Public sports centre with a 9.8 x 4.2 m climbing wall; FEDPE runs youth workshops (Verano 2025, Av. del Aire cdra 9, San Luis). Bouldering not clearly stated; kept out.
