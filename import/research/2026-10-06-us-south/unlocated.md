# USA South: real venues without a usable coordinate (2026-10-06)

No candidate was created for these: the coordinate rules (own OSM element, Nominatim house-number geocode of the official address, or the gym's own map pin) did not give a trustworthy pin. Each is listed with what was found; an owner pin or a later geocode would turn them into candidates.

1. Fort Rock Climbing Center, Fort Myers FL. 7131 Alico Rd, Suite 130, Fort Myers, FL 33912. Site https://fortrockclimbing.com/
   Offer: indoor-bouldering, top-rope. No usable coordinate: Nominatim finds no house-number match for 7131 Alico Road (the top result is a quarry); the own-site pin (26.5236, -81.809, 4 decimals) reverse-geocodes to a house on Cannongate Drive, so it was not used.
   Evidence: Official site (2026-10-06): "a 5,000 sq ft climbing center and a 5,000 sq ft indoor playground", menu: Auto-belay climbing, Top-rope climbing, Bouldering; passes, memberships and classes; site data gives 7131 Alico Road, Suite 130 and its own coordinates (26.5236, -81.809). A different gym from Central Rock Gym Fort Myers (about 5 km apart).
2. Stone Climbing – St. Augustine, St. Augustine FL. 115 Strongway Ct, St. Augustine, FL 32086. Site https://www.stoneclimbing.com/
   Offer: indoor-bouldering, top-rope. No usable coordinate: Nominatim has no entry for 115 Strongway Court and the site embeds no map pin.
   Evidence: Official site (2026-10-06): "full-service climbing gym offering rope climbing, bouldering, fitness, yoga, and youth programs", day passes and memberships with "access to both Stone locations", Intro to Bouldering class; lists St. Augustine at 115 Strongway Court.
3. Sun Country Rocks, Jonesville FL. 333 SW 140th Terrace, Jonesville, FL 32669. Site https://suncountrysports.com/suncountryrocks/
   Offer: indoor-bouldering, top-rope, lead-climbing. No usable coordinate: Nominatim has no entry for 333 SW 140th Terrace; the site only links a Google Maps search by address.
   Evidence: Rock wall inside the Sun Country Sports Center (Walltopia "Fun Walls"): "Top-rope climbing, lead climbing and bouldering are available", youth rec classes, party packages; hours of the centre are published. No page found about public day entry or admission.
4. Wilmington Rock Gym, Wilmington NC. 8118 Market St, Wilmington, NC 28411. Site https://www.wilmingtonrockgym.com/
   Offer: indoor-bouldering, top-rope. No usable coordinate: Nominatim only matches Market Street at street level, and the pin embedded in the site (34.2943, -77.7922) reverse-geocodes to Marsh Reach Drive, not the gym.
   Evidence: Official site (2026-10-06): "50+ top rope routes and 60+ bouldering routes", hours-and-rates page with day pass "top rope routes and bouldering", membership prices.
