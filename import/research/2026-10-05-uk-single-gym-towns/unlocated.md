# UK: real bouldering venues without a usable pin or address (2026-10-05)

No candidate was created for these because the gym's own site did not give an address that could be geocoded to the building, and no OSM element was found. Nothing here is a decision; they are leads for a later pass.

1. Dyno Climbing Centre, Buckfastleigh (Devon). Independent centre with roped walls and a dedicated bouldering room (site https://www.dynoclimbingcentre.co.uk/, hours page https://www.dynoclimbingcentre.co.uk/Opening-Hours). The site's contact page has a phone number but no street address; no OSM element in the extract.
2. The Climbing Station, Loughborough (Leicestershire). Bouldering centre with online bookings and opening hours (https://www.theclimbingstation.com/); the site pages read give no street address, and its footer year is 2012-2018. Third-party listings give Empress Road (LE11 1RH), which Nominatim only resolves to the street.
3. The Climbing Experience (G13 Bouldering), Maidstone (Kent). Official site https://theclimbingexperience.co.uk/ shows bouldering, hours and "Unit G13, Baltic Wharf, St Peter's Street, Maidstone ME16 0ST", but Nominatim does not resolve Baltic Wharf and returns a different St Peter's Street in Ringlestone for the postcode query, so no safe pin.
4. Climb Kent, Maidstone (Lower Grange Farm, Sandling). Listed by directories only; its own site was not read and it may be roped-only.
