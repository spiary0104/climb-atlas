# Unlocated (Turkiye): real gyms with no usable coordinate source

No OSM element, official-site map pin or chain list coordinates were found for these. They need a later pass (e.g. a map pin from the gym's Instagram or Google profile).

1. **Mekanik Climbing**, Izmir (Bornova, Ozkanlar). Described as a boulder gym with colour-coded problems, five fingerboards and a MoonBoard. Only source is a 2019 page on a club member's blog (http://www.demirayaklar.com/mekanik-climbing/); it has no map, no site of its own was found, and current opening status is unknown (the page gives 2019-era hours).
2. **Bursa Tirmanis Evi** (run by a Bursa mountaineering club), Bursa (Osmangazi). Directory listings (https://www.indoorclimbing.com/turkey.html) give an address in Ahmetpasa Mah., about 60 m2 of angled bouldering wall, open a few evenings a week. No official site, no map pin; the directory entry may be old. A members' club, so category would be "club".
3. **Duvar (Bursa)**, Bursa. A volunteer-founded (2009) non-profit climbing gym with a Facebook page (https://www.facebook.com/duvarbursa/, not readable here). No address, hours or bouldering confirmation obtained.
4. **911 Search & Rescue Association wall**, Bursa (Osmangazi). Directory-only mention of an 18 m2 adjustable wall; a rescue-association facility, access to the public unclear, no map.
