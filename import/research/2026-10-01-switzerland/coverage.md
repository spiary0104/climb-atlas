# Switzerland coverage (wave 1, 2026-10-01)

## Result
87 candidates in candidates.ndjson (check.js: ready 41 | review 44 | blocked 2 | existing 0 | invalid 0), 71 registered sources, 11 real gyms in unlocated.md.
The two blocked ones are deliberate: Mitō Bouldering Zürich (site words it as still being built but lists hours; marked opening-soon) and BoulderBurg Burgdorf (site announces opening 17-18 Oct 2026).
Review flags are mostly chain siblings sharing one website (TOTEM, Grimper.ch, Minimum, Boulderlounge, Gaswerk, Kraftreaktor), club halls (limited-access) and probable matches with seed spots; none were changed.

## Cities / regions covered
Zürich (Minimum x2, GrindelBoulder, Gaswerk x3, Boulderlounge Schlieren, Adliswil, Uster, Winterthur x2), Schaffhausen, Thurgau, St. Gallen (Boulderlounge, Kletterzentrum, Quergang, Sparta Buchs, Boulder Box), Appenzell AR, Glarus, Graubünden (Chur, Domat/Ems, Küblis), Bern (city, Thun, Steffisburg, Worb, Schwarzenburg, Lenk, Langenthal, Meiringen, Biel, Niederwangen, Sumiswald, Langnau, Ostermundigen, Burgdorf), Aargau (Baden, Lenzburg, Aarau, Windisch, Aarburg), Solothurn, Basel-Stadt and Basel-Landschaft, Luzern (Kriens, Root, Sursee, Ettiswil, Wolhusen), Zug, Schwyz, Uri, Fribourg, Vaud, Geneva, Valais, Neuchâtel, Jura, Ticino (Giubiasco only).

## Main sources
Gym and chain sites (primary), OSM extract for coordinates, plus leads from the VSBK/ASSE member list (kletteranlagen.ch), kletterportal.ch canton lists, indoorclimbing.com, torpille.ch and the Redpoint gym list. ClimbDirectory and the SAC list were reachable only as teasers (no gym names) so were not used for evidence. Directories were used as leads only.

## Likely missing / weak spots
- Ticino is thin: only Alpha Boulder is included. Evolution Climbing Center (Taverne/Torricella) could not be verified (site blocks access; one news item reported closure, another reopening in 2026). Palestra Audan (Ambrì, SAT Ritom club) and the Lugano-Cornaredo hall were seen only on tourism/directory pages (no primary source) so were left out.
- Valais and Romandie beyond the chains: searches were cut short (web search quota ran out), so small independent salles in Sion, Martigny, La Chaux-de-Fonds, Nyon, Morges, Lausanne suburbs and the Jura were not canvassed. Jura/Neuchâtel/Valais independents are the most probable gap. Possible leads not verified: Devers (Mettembert) and Die Boulderhalle Dübendorf (both from the Redpoint list, sites do not resolve), Blocstock (Bressaucourt, OSM only), Boulderpark Albigna/Pé d'Maira (Bregaglia, likely outdoor).
- Gyms with weaker bouldering evidence are included but noted in research_notes: Climbox Langnau, Escalade Chavornay, L'Entrepôt Bulle, Magnet, Greifensee (site mentions outdoor boulders).
- Excluded on purpose (not public, closed, or not indoor): Boulder-Buchlern Zürich (outdoor), ASVZ walls (members), Hirslen Bülach (seasonal hall, indoor status unclear), Birsmill Boulder Laufen (club hall, mostly members, coordinates unclear), Boulder-Eggä Lichtensteig (members only), Kletterwand Wil/Bergholz, Freiruum Zug BoulderArea (leaving its site), Sargans, Interlaken K44, Sporttreff Zihl Nidau, boulder-gade Arth (all reported closed/liquidated), Klettertreff Thun (no bouldering shown on its site), Davos Färbi, Raiver (Domat club wall), ASEN Neuchâtel and Genève Montagne (no bouldering shown), Sport-Escalade Villeneuve (club in the Grimper.ch hall), municipal sports centres whose wall counts as category "other" (Queue d'Arve in Geneva, Centro POLI in Olivone), school/hotel walls.
- Several chain/regional sites list more rope-only gyms that were not recorded.
