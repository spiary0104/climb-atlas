Open status rests on gym-authored 2026 posts, live booking and a current price list; visitor reviews dated 2026-07 to 2026-10 are only corroboration. Pins are the gym's own Naver pin (`map-service-pin`, building), cross-checked with Kakao Map: all accepts are within 61 m of the Kakao pin (most within 20 m; Kindy 61 m and Grippick 60 m are the widest, same building).
