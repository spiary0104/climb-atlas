- 48 candidates (jp-fk/ng/km/oi/mz/ks/ok/hk/my/hs/oy), 47 accepted for staging, 1 deferred (Panda Wall). Every accepted gym has its own site and/or its own Instagram (public embed, posts dated Aug-Oct 2026) or a chain store list; rockgym.jp (directory) was used only for names and as supporting evidence.
- 87 sources registered (one per gym site, Instagram account, GSI, directory).
- Coordinates: GSI address search (house-number or parcel level) for 46; 2 pins are the centre of the gym's own Google Maps embed (district-level GSI result: Wow'd+ Kiyotake, Nekonote Soja); 5 pins are street/block-level and flagged.

## Method notes
- Names from rockgym.jp prefecture lists (12 prefectures, ~115 entries), tokukita.jp/bouldering-link.com for Hokkaido, and web searches; WebSearch quota ran out mid-session, so no further discovery was done beyond those lists.
- Recency evidence: gyms' public Instagram embeds (https://www.instagram.com/<handle>/embed/, post timestamps read 2026-10-06), own-site dated news, calendars, sitemap/feed dates, chain store lists. Directory-only status was never trusted: it was wrong for Rock Party, K-WALL's operator, Family Wall, NAC Sapporo, Gravity Research (Sapporo, Okayama), Zi;BOX, Smoothie, Korupiki, Top Out.
- Chains covered: OD (Kokura, Yahata, Munakata; Kokura Station closed), Mono Climbing Studio (Omura, Sasebo; Hakata existing), Climbing Park (Kikunan, Higashihiroshima), Bravo Climbing (Nagasaki only), rocks (Okayama + Kurashiki), B'nuts (Furukawa; Sendai existing), D.Bouldering (all five stores in this area were already in Bouldeer). B-PUMP, Base Camp, NOBOROCK, ROCKY and Gravity Research have no open store in these prefectures.
- Access problems: kotih.com, b-smoothie.com, big-foot.asia, pb-climbing.com return 403/503 to our IP; X and Facebook are not readable; several own sites are JavaScript-only or undated.
