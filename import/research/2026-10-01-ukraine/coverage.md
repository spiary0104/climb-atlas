# Coverage: Ukraine (UA), wave 3A, 2026-10-01

## Result
16 candidates (check.js: ready 0, review 5, blocked 9, existing 2, invalid 0) and 7 unlocated gyms. Ready is 0 because most official sites carry no dated 2025-2026 sign of operation (wartime), so status_claim is "unknown" for 9 candidates.

## Cities covered
Kyiv (Hyperion, UP!, Funattic, KPI Climbing Club, The Wall Kyiv, Ostriv; unlocated: Climbing SPACE, Boulder Space, NAU), Lviv (The Wall Lviv, Bukhta; unlocated: Skelia), Kharkiv (MurawayNick, FormAT), Dnipro (Montana, La Scala, Skala), Zaporizhzhia (Energy Wall), Ternopil (Climbing Room), Odesa (SK Dynamica; unlocated: ShelRocks), Poltava (unlocated: ON-SITE), Vinnytsia (unlocated: CaFa).

## Main sources
Gyms' own sites (Wix, WordPress and static sites fetched directly), OSM Overpass extract UA.json (132 elements, mostly outdoor crags and rope parks), the Kyiv guide at nashkiev.ua (2023), the Dnipro overview at nashemisto.dp.ua (2025-01-31), city directories (dityvmisti, list.in.ua) as leads only. Web-search quota ran out partway; later checks were done by direct page fetches.
