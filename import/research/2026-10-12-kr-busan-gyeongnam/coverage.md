## Method and limits
Kakao Map place search (about 650 queries) plus place panels (pins, entrance points, blog dates) for every Kakao hit in the three provinces; Naver Place list search (about 40 queries; the list endpoint then returned HTTP 429, so further Naver list queries were dropped) and every candidate's own Naver Place home and feed pages. Owner photos were inspected as montages (the Naver home page exposes about four owner photos per listing). Naver's map search API is behind a captcha and was not used. Instagram, Facebook and Kakao Talk were not readable. Not covered: gyms that exist only on Instagram or Kakao Talk, anything opened after mid-2026 that search has not indexed, and gyms whose Naver/Kakao names do not contain a climbing word (searches also used boulder, wall, rock and chain names).
