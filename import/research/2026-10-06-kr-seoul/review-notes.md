## Method
Candidates were found with Naver Place list search and Kakao Map search (city, district, station and chain queries), then each gym's own Naver Place listing was read (price list, booking, gym-authored posts) and its owner-uploaded photos were inspected (montage per gym). Bouldering is accepted only where the gym's own photos show mat-floored bouldering walls (or its own post says so). Open status rests on gym-authored 2026 posts, a live booking item and price list; visitor reviews dated 2026-09/10 are only corroboration. Pins are the gym's own Naver pin (`map-service-pin`, building), cross-checked with Kakao (most within a few metres).
