## Method
- Chain location lists first: Movement (location-finder JSON with its own coordinates for every gym), The Spot, Bouldering Project (location sitemap: no Chicago, Denver or Dallas gym), First Ascent (via Wayback), Momentum, Crux, Armadillo, inSPIRE (Spring, Cypress, Lubbock only), Mesa Rim, Austin Bouldering Project, Vertical Endeavors (site blocked).
- Independents and openings: Overpass `sport=climbing` for 8 bounding boxes (OSM tags few gyms: only a handful of names the directories did not already show), Mountain Project, IndoorClimbingGym and 99Boulders state directories (leads only, never evidence), Climbing Business Journal gym profiles and searches (found Crux South Austin, MoCo, Lake Travis, Blok, Horizon), KUT's 2026 Austin guide, 2026 city guides.
- Coordinates: OSM building ways for addresses (Nominatim/Photon), OSM way of the gym itself (First Ascent West Loop), and the gym's own embedded Google map (Momentum Memorial). Movement's own map data was used to check the existing Movement pins.
- The Overpass name-regex queries never completed (shared quota, timeouts), and Yelp/DuckDuckGo/Bing could not be scraped; discovery by name relied on the sources above.
