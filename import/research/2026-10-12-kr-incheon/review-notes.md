## Method
Candidates were found with Naver Place list search (about 150 queries: city, district, dong, station and chain names plus bouldering/climbing/wall keywords) and Kakao Map search (about 100 queries). Each gym's own Naver Place home and feed pages were read through the page state (price list, booking items, gym-authored posts, newest visitor reviews) and its owner-uploaded photos were inspected as a contact sheet. Bouldering is accepted only where the gym's own photos show mat-floored bouldering walls. Open status rests on gym-authored 2026 posts, live booking and a current price list; visitor reviews are corroboration. Pins are the gym's own Naver pin cross-checked with Kakao (all within 1-17 m). Incheon was reorganised in 2026: addresses now read Geomdan-gu / Seohae-gu (old Seo-gu) and Yeongjong-gu (old Jung-gu); the state stays INCHEON.
