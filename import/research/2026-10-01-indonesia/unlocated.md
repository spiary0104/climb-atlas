# Indonesia: real gyms without a usable coordinate source (2026-10-01)

No OSM feature and no official-site pin/coordinate was found for these. Details are for a later pass (e.g. a map-pin check or a gym-published pin).
Status/bouldering notes say what was actually seen on a primary page; "social only" = Instagram/Facebook profile existence, no readable post content.

## Likely open with bouldering (priority for a later pass)

1. Dreamstone Boulders Kelapa Gading (Jakarta Timur, DKI_JAKARTA). Komplek Gading Festival, Jl. Sedayu Boulevard Raya No. 1, Cakung. Official page says largest bouldering gym in North Jakarta (about 1,000 sqm, Kilter Board), open 10:00-22:00 daily. The site's location API returns empty lat/long for this branch.
   https://dreamstoneboulders.com/kelapagading , https://dreamstoneboulders.com/location
2. Rock Island Bali (Pererenan, Mengwi, Badung, BALI). Jl. Sempol No. 25. Official site: boulder, top rope and lead, hours 10:00-21:00 daily. Contact page map is a text query only, no pin.
   https://rockisland.id/ , https://rockisland.id/contact-us , https://www.instagram.com/rockisland.id/
3. Alpine Outpost (Jakarta Pusat). JB Tower, Jl. Kebon Sirih No. 48-50, 6th floor of the parking building. Directories and social describe a boulder-only gym; social only, no website found. (The Facebook page named alpineoutpost shows Chiang Mai, a different business.)
   https://www.instagram.com/alpine_outpost_indonesia/ , https://www.tiktok.com/@alpine_outpost_indonesia
4. Climb ON Indoor Climbing Gym (Jakarta). Search results give Plaza Semanggi, 1st floor (opened 2020); an older directory entry says Menteng Huis, so the address needs checking. Boulder walls plus some rope routes. Social only.
   https://www.instagram.com/climbon.official/
5. Manjat Climbing Gym / Yuk Manjat (Bandung, JAWA_BARAT). Click Square mall, Jl. Naripan No. 89. Listings mention lead, speed, boulder and moonboard. Social only.
   https://www.instagram.com/yukmanjat/
6. Peak to Peak Indoor Climbing (Tangerang Selatan, BANTEN). Ruko Alam Sutera 1 No. 18, Pakulonan, Serpong Utara. Boulder section on 2nd floor per listings. Own site peaktopeakclimbing.com blocked us (Wordfence 503), so nothing could be read.
   https://peaktopeakclimbing.com/
7. Ape Boulder Camp (Tangerang, Buaran Indah, BANTEN). Gg. Pelopor No. 1; MoonBoard and Kilter Board per listings. Social only (an @apeboulder.camp guess returned no profile).
8. Klimb Boulder Gym Bali (Canggu, BALI). Jl. Raya Babakan No. 30; opened 1 Sept 2023 per listings. Website klimbbali.com is only an admin CRM login. Instagram @klimb.bali exists.
   https://www.instagram.com/klimb.bali/
9. Monk Climbing Gym (Peliatan, Ubud, BALI). Jl. Raya Andong No. 89. Official site calls it a boulder climbing gym; map embed is a text query only. Probably the successor of Tokei Ubud (same street, directory listed Tokei at Jl. Raya Andong).
   https://monkclimbinggym.com/
10. Boulder House Indonesia (Makassar, SULAWESI_SELATAN). Jl. Pengayoman, Ruko Alfa No. 38; a post says it opened early in the year (new). Social only.
    https://www.instagram.com/boulderhouse.id/
11. RC Boulder (Medan, SUMATERA_UTARA). Described as the first dedicated bouldering space in Medan; booking via Instagram. Address not confirmed.
    https://www.instagram.com/rcboulder/
12. Phyxius Climbing Gym (Surabaya, JAWA_TIMUR). Jl. Dukuh Kupang Timur X-B No. 21 (some listings XI/21B). Three floors with boulder/roof areas plus tall rope walls, est. 2005; listings give odd short hours. Social only.
    https://www.instagram.com/phyxius.climbing.gym/ , https://www.facebook.com/widiartoerwin/

## Unclear status or doubtful (low priority)

13. Bremgra Indoor Climbing Gym (Serpong, Tangerang Selatan, BANTEN). Jl. Cilenggang Raya No. 123. Mostly rope walls with some boulder problems per a directory; bremgraindonesia.com did not connect. Bouldering would be unknown.
14. Apetorium (Ngaglik, Sleman, DI_YOGYAKARTA). Sports studio with a bouldering wall (calisthenics + bouldering), hours listed on aggregators. Facebook only. Borderline (small, studio).
    https://www.facebook.com/people/Apetorium/100046239647309/
15. Climbing Camp Semarang (JAWA_TENGAH). Facebook page exists (https://www.facebook.com/climbingcamp.id/); disciplines and address unverified.
16. Fitriyani Climbing Arena (Sleman, DI_YOGYAKARTA). Listed on a blog only; not verified.
17. Badung Climbing Gym (Seminyak, BALI). Jl. Kunti No. 2; directory lists it as 6 boulder blocks, speed and lead wall, apparently outdoor, random hours. Not verified.
18. Tokei Ubud Climbing Gym (BALI). Reported permanently closed on aggregators; Facebook page https://www.facebook.com/Tokeiubud/ still exists. See Monk Climbing Gym.
19. Class 5 Recreational Climbing Center (Surabaya; OSM node/5059098926 "Class5 climbing"). Reported permanently closed on TripAdvisor; its site is an old WordPress blog. Not made a candidate (closed, no primary source for current status).
20. Ismuadinagoro Climbing Gym (Yogyakarta). Described as a private home bouldering wall, not a public gym.
21. University / club walls (MAPAGAMA UGM boulder wall, Wall Climbing UIN Sunan Kalijaga, UNPAD, Kappa UI, ITS): members/students, no public offer confirmed; OSM has several unnamed sports_centre/climbing elements around Jakarta and Bandung that could not be tied to a named gym.
22. AE Climbing Gym (Bandung, Jl. Sadagori 243b) and Rebel Gym at Piset Mall (Bandung; treadwall): directory only, no recent source; likely rope or not bouldering.
