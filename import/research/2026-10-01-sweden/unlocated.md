# Sweden: real indoor bouldering venues without a coordinate source (2026-10-01)

Each entry: bouldering offer seen on the venue's own site unless noted. No OSM element in the extract and no map pin/coordinates published on the site
(checked the pages listed), so no candidate was created. Addresses are as published.

## Commercial / institutional

1. Sundsvalls Klätterhall, Sundsvall (VASTERNORRLAND). Universitetsallén 30, 852 34 Sundsvall.
   Site https://www.xn--sundsvallskltterhall-mzb.se/ (sundsvallsklätterhall.se). Evidence: https://www.xn--sundsvallskltterhall-mzb.se/klattra-hos-oss/ (bouldering walls 2-4.5 m plus rope walls, run with Sundsvalls Klätterklubb).
2. IKSU Sport, klätterhallen Lofoten, Umeå (VASTERBOTTEN). Rope + bouldering hall in IKSU Sport (opened 2018).
   Site https://www.iksu.se/sektioner/klattring/om-iksu-klattring/ . Evidence: that page (club climbs boulder and lead); hall details from https://www.mynewsdesk.com/se/iksu/pressreleases/18-slash-11-oeppnar-klaetterhallen-lofoten-paa-iksu-sport-2790369 (news, not primary).
3. KFUM Umeå Klättring och bouldering, Umeå (VASTERBOTTEN). Järnvägsallén 20.
   Site https://umea.kfum.se/ . Evidence: https://umea.kfum.se/klattervaggen/klattring-och-bouldering/ (bouldering hall; public drop-in days announced on the homepage).
4. KFUM Skellefteå klättervägg, Skellefteå (VASTERBOTTEN).
   Site https://skelleftea.kfum.se/ . Evidence: https://skelleftea.kfum.se/start/?ID=317931 (rope climbing and bouldering walls).
5. Grepp, Stenkullen / Lerum (VASTRA_GOTALAND). Smedvägen 24, 443 61 Stenkullen.
   Site https://grepp.se/ . Evidence: https://grepp.se/ (16 m rope walls and bouldering, junior section).
6. Beta Boulders Göteborg (VASTRA_GOTALAND). Announced, not yet open; no address published (site runs a guess-the-location contest).
   Site https://betaboulders.se/goteborg/ (status opening-soon; will need a pass once it opens).
7. Campus1477 Blåsenhus, Uppsala (UPPSALA). Fitness gym with a bouldering wall by the entrance (borderline: fitness gym, not a dedicated climbing gym).
   Site https://campus1477.se/en/training/climbing/boulder-climbing/ .
8. Kroppslabbet, Lidköping (VASTRA_GOTALAND). Sockerbruksgatan 3c, 531 40 Lidköping. Health club with bouldering walls (borderline: fitness gym).
   Site https://kroppslabbet.se/klattring/ . Evidence: that page.
