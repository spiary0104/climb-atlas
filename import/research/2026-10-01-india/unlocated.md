# Unlocated / unconfirmed leads (India)

Real gyms, or likely gyms, with no usable coordinate source (not in the OSM extract, no map pin on the gym's own site), or where bouldering or open status is unconfirmed. Not written as candidates.

## Real gyms, bouldering confirmed on own site, no coordinates

1. **Lets Play Climbing**, Bengaluru (KARNATAKA). KSFC Bhavan, #1/1 Thimmaiah Rd, Vasanth Nagar, 560052.
   Site: https://www.letsplayclimbing.com/ (states a bouldering focus; no map or coordinates on the page; not in OSM).
   Socials: https://www.instagram.com/letsplayclimbing/ and https://www.facebook.com/letsplayclimbing/
2. **Urban Climbers - The Boulder Project**, Haralur / Bellandur, Bengaluru (KARNATAKA). Near Kaikondrahalli Lake Trail, opposite Cult.fit, Ambalipura, Haralur 560035.
   Site: https://urbanclimbers.in/ (sport climbing / bouldering, day pass listed; no address or map on the site).
   Lead only: the Hudle booking page https://hudle.in/venues/urban-climbers-the-boulder-project/730733 lists 12.915189, 77.6729537 (third-party platform, not accepted as a coordinate source). An OSM node "Rock Climbing" (node/3632677649, 12.9116566, 77.6753202) is about 450 m away and cannot be tied to this gym.
3. **On The Rocks**, Mulund West, Mumbai (MAHARASHTRA). Premises No. 61, Minerva Industrial Estate, opp. Marigold Building, P.K. Road, 400081. Dedicated bouldering zone, hours shown (12:00-21:00).
   Site: https://ontherocksmumbai.com/ (no map pin; the structured data carries a template Swiss coordinate, ignore it); Instagram https://www.instagram.com/ontherocksmumbai
4. **Girivihar - YMCA bouldering wall**, Grant Road East, Mumbai (MAHARASHTRA). Bombay YMCA Student Branch, Kadam Marg. Girivihar runs beginner bouldering workshops there (INR 1000, 2 hours).
   Evidence: https://www.girivihar.org/ymca-bouldering-workshop and https://www.girivihar.org/climbing (says the wall is moving from Colaba to Grant Road). No map pin; not in OSM. Open-to-public walk-in status not shown.
