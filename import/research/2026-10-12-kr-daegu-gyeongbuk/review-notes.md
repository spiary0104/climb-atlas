## Method

Each gym's own Naver Place listing was then read (home and feed tabs: price list, booking, gym-authored posts with dates, owner photos, dated visitor photo reviews). Owner photos were downloaded and inspected as montages. Bouldering is accepted only where the gym's own photos show mat-floored boulder walls (or its own post says so); open status rests on gym-authored 2026 posts, a live booking item or the price list, with 2026 visitor reviews only as corroboration. Pins are the gym's own Naver Place pin (`map-service-pin`, building), cross-checked with Kakao: all 37 cross-checked pins are within 0 to 17 m, apart from three public facilities (37 to 56 m, deferred anyway).
