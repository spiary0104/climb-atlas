## Method
- Four parallel read-only fact sweeps (München/Oberbayern; Franken/Oberpfalz/Niederbayern/Schwaben; Stuttgart + Karlsruhe/Rhein-Neckar + Mittelbaden; Südbaden/Bodensee/Oberschwaben). Each gym was confirmed on its own site (hours, a bouldering mention, street address); DAV section sites and chain sites count as official. Directories (kletterszene, boulderhallenfinder, outdooractive, tourism sites) were used for leads only.
- All web searches of the session ran out partway (shared budget of 200), so smaller towns were covered only by directory lists (see remaining leads).
- Pins: the gym's own OSM element or the OSM element at the official house number, found with Photon (komoot, OSM data) at 1 request per second. 58 pins are OSM elements named after the gym; 9 are the OSM element at the official house number (method geocoded-address).
