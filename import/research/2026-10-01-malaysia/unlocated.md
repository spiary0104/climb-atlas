# Unlocated: real gyms with no coordinate source yet (Malaysia)

No OSM element for the gym was found (extract plus a few OSM API bounding-box checks), and the gym's own site publishes no pin or coordinates. They need the separate geocoding pass. "Primary" = the gym's own site or chain list.

## Primary evidence of bouldering found (only coordinates missing)

1. Camp5 Utropolis (chain Camp5; boulder-only gym), Shah Alam, Selangor. Lot UG-D1A/B/C, Utropolis Marketplace, Jalan Kontraktor U1/14, Seksyen U1, 40150 Shah Alam. Site: https://www.camp5.com/utropolis/ (350 sqm bouldering, hours listed, current posts Oct 2026).
2. Camp5 Paradigm, Johor Bahru, Johor. 7F-08, Level 7, Paradigm Mall, Jalan Skudai, 81200 Johor Bahru. Site: https://www.camp5.com/paradigm/ (bouldering plus lead, top-rope, autobelay; opened 2017). Note: the one unnamed OSM climbing way in Johor Bahru (way/1493012062) sits beside a skate park on Jalan Tun Abdul Razak, not at Paradigm, so it was not used.
3. Camp5 KL East, Kuala Lumpur. Level G3 and L1, KL East Mall, Jalan Lingkaran Tengah 2, 53100 Kuala Lumpur. Site: https://www.camp5.com/kleast/ (bouldering, 12 m top-rope, lead; Play Climb zone).
4. MadMonkeyz Climbing Gym, Wangsa Maju, Kuala Lumpur. 19-1 and 19-2, Jalan Metro Wangsa, 53300 Kuala Lumpur. Site: https://madmonkeyz.my/ and https://madmonkeyz.my/contact/ (bouldering FAQ, price list; site footer 2026). The site's map embed is a text query only, no coordinates.
5. Batuu Climbing, Damansara Utama, Petaling Jaya, Selangor. G-28, Ground Floor, 3 Damansara, Jalan SS 20/27 (address from directories and event pages, not from the gym site). Site: https://www.batuuclimbing.com/about (bouldering, top rope and lead, founded Sep 2024).
6. Rocky Basecamp AEON Mall Tebrau City, Johor Bahru, Johor. Lot F102B, Level 1, AEON Mall Tebrau City, 1 Jalan Desa Tebrau, 81100 Johor Bahru. Site: https://rockybasecamp.com.my/contacts/ and https://rockybasecamp.com.my/about-us/ (mentions bouldering challenges; family and speed-climbing focus). Map embed is a text query only.
7. Rocky Basecamp AEON Mall Shah Alam, Selangor. Lot T05, Level 3, Jalan Akuatik 13/64, Seksyen 13, 40100 Shah Alam. Same site and pages as above. Unclear how much bouldering it has; reviewer should decide whether it counts as a climbing gym or a kids' adventure park.
