# Review notes: Korea central regions (2026-10-12-kr-central-regions)

Reviewer: Claude (AI) on the owner's instruction; owner sign-off pending. Tally: accept 15 | same-as 25 | defer 14 | reject 0 (54 candidates).

## Method
Candidates were found with the Naver Place list search (the same query the Naver app sends: 4 keywords x about 60 cities and districts, then dong-level, chain-name and name-word queries) and the Kakao Map search (about 5 keywords x the same cities), then each gym's own Naver Place listing was read: intro text (정보 tab), price list, booking flag, gym-authored posts (소식 tab) and the owner-uploaded photos (montage per gym). Bouldering is accepted only where the gym's own photos show mat-floored boulder walls or its own text says so (intro, keywords, a post). Open status rests on gym-authored 2026 posts, live booking and a current price list; visitor photos dated 2026-09/10 are only corroboration. Pins are the gym's own Naver pin, cross-checked with the Kakao pin of the same place (all accepted gyms within 20 m, except ClimbMax Cheonan 72 m, see below); Google coordinates were not used.
