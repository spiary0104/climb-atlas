## Method
1. Names only from directories: rockgym.jp prefecture pages (about 114 gyms in the 10 prefectures), cross-checks on the Nagoya list at yamahack.com, fitmap.jp, climbers-web.jp and web searches by city for gyms missing from rockgym (Mie, Nagoya, Niigata, Nagano).
2. Every accepted gym was read on its own official page and/or its official Instagram. Instagram evidence is the public embed of the account (`instagram.com/<user>/embed/`), which lists the latest posts with timestamps and captions; dates in each candidate's research_notes are those latest-post dates, read 2026-10-06. Every accepted gym has an official Instagram post dated 2026-08-15 or later, or dated 2026 news/calendar on its own site (checked by script against the candidate evidence).
3. Coordinates: GSI address search (house or parcel level, `building`) for 50 gyms, block level (`street`, flagged) for 3 (Cloud Nine, Planet Fukuroi, Sunny Rock Numazu), OSM elements for 3 (Sunny Space Iwanchu Toyokawa, Square Climbing Center, Ikeda wall). Gyms where GSI returned only the district and no OSM element exists were NOT accepted (to9-gamagori, reboulder-kanayama, crag-hamakita, pirania-minami-alps: pin only is missing).
4. Chains named in the brief: B-PUMP/PUMP (Tokyo and Osaka region only), Base Camp, NOBOROCK, Rocky (Chiba, Tokyo), Gravity Research, T-WALL: no store in these prefectures could be found in their store lists or searches except Gravity Research Niigata (not verified open, see leads). Chubu chains swept from their own pages: Climbing JAM (3 shops, all accepted), Pinnacle 2 (Toyota, Obu accepted; Sakae existing), Cuore (2 shops), Sunny Rock/Blue Canyon (3), BUBU (3), Gyodo (2 Gifu/Aichi shops accepted, Ichinomiya and Kakamigahara), Bould TO9 (Shinshiro accepted, Gamagori pin-only), Pirania (Isawa accepted), Play Mountain (Okazaki, and Nagoya IC now trading as climbing.gym RAY), Sunny Space Iwanchu (2), Planet (Fukuroi; Shizuoka shop closed), KO-WALL (Mie accepted; the other KO-WALL shops are in Shiga, out of scope).
