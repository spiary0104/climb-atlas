Blocat: not confirmed bouldering.

## Method
- Leads from directories (rocodromos.com, rocodromos.net, Mountain Project; only leads). Every accepted gym has its own site (or the chain page) read on 2026-10-06 for exists, open signs (hours, tariffs, dated news) and bouldering. Coordinates: the gym own OSM element, its own map link or embed, or a geocode of the official address (5 pins are street-level and flagged).
- Tools: page text was read with a summarising fetch tool and, where it said nothing about bouldering, by grepping the raw page text.
