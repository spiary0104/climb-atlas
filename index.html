About Boulder Atlas

A community-sourced map of Australian & American bouldering & climbing spots

Boulder Atlas is a free, crowd-built map of places to climb across Australia and the United States — indoor bouldering gyms and gyms with top-rope and lead walls. It's built in the spirit of sites like Track Atlas: instead of one team trying to track every gym forever, the map is kept current by the people who actually climb at these places.

How the map works

Each pin is colour-coded by what's on offer there — indoor bouldering or top rope. A pin split into wedges means a spot offers more than one. Use the checkboxes to filter by country/state, by type, or by your own climbed/bookmarked marks.

Where the data comes from

The initial set of spots was researched and cross-checked gym by gym — names, suburbs, and what kind of climbing is actually on offer — rather than pulled from one source and trusted blindly. It's still not exhaustive, and gyms open, close, move, and rebrand. From here, the map grows and gets corrected by the community.

Adding & fixing spots

  • Add a spot — drop a pin for any gym that's missing.
  • Edit this spot — on any pin, fix the name, location, climbing type, or add a photo link.

New spots and edits go into a review queue — a moderator approves or rejects them before they're visible to everyone else. An edited spot keeps showing its current info on the map until that edit is approved, so nothing unreviewed goes live in the meantime.

Tracking your own climbing

Sign in (Google or an email magic link) to mark spots as climbed or bookmarked. These marks are private to your account — nobody else can see what you've climbed or saved.

What we collect

Two kinds of data: (1) Anything voluntarily entered into the "Add a spot" or "Edit this spot" forms — gym/crag details such as name, location, climbing type, notes, and an optional photo link. (2) If you sign in, your email address (and, for Google sign-in, whatever basic profile info Google shares) plus which spots you've privately marked as climbed or bookmarked.

How submitted information is used

Anything submitted through the "Add a spot" / "Edit this spot" forms is displayed publicly to all visitors — that's the whole point of a shared map. Don't include personal information in a submission that you don't want shown publicly. Climbed/bookmarked marks are private and tied to your account — only you can see them.

Third parties

Map tiles are served by OpenStreetMap and CARTO. Loading the map means your browser requests tiles from their servers directly; see their own privacy policies for how they handle that. Accounts and data storage are handled by Supabase; Google sign-in is handled by Google. See their respective privacy policies for how they handle your data.
