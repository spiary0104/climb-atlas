Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

What we collect

Three kinds of data: (1) Anything voluntarily entered into the "Add a spot" or "Edit this spot" forms — gym/crag details such as name, location, address, climbing type, notes, and an optional photo link. (2) Anything entered into a "Report incorrect information" form — just a free-text message about a specific spot. (3) If you sign in, your email address (and, for Google sign-in, whatever basic profile info Google shares) plus which spots you've privately marked as climbed or bookmarked.

How submitted information is used

Anything submitted through the "Add a spot" / "Edit this spot" forms is displayed publicly to all visitors once a moderator approves it — that's the whole point of a shared map. Don't include personal information in a submission that you don't want shown publicly. "Report incorrect information" messages are different — they're only ever visible to moderators, reviewed to decide whether the spot needs correcting, and not displayed on the public map. Climbed/bookmarked marks are private and tied to your account — only you can see them.

Third parties

Map tiles are served by OpenStreetMap and CARTO. Loading the map means your browser requests tiles from their servers directly; see their own privacy policies for how they handle that. Accounts and data storage are handled by Supabase; Google sign-in is handled by Google. See their respective privacy policies for how they handle your data.

Requesting removal

To have a submission corrected or removed, use the Edit function on the relevant pin, or contact climbatlas0104@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site adds features like accounts, analytics, or advertising. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
